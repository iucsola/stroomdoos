The Primer beta.

This guide is for beta testers. Primer puts your multitrack WAVs into Logic Pro in one batch: one track per file, named, coloured and routed to an aux per group.

The beta is invite-only. Not a tester? Join the mailing list and we'll tell you when Primer is out.

What you need

  • A Mac with macOS 14 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel.
  • Logic Pro 12 or 13, set to English.
  • Multitrack WAV files. A few files or a full session.

Install

  1. Open the .dmg. Drag Primer to Applications.
  2. Open Primer from Applications. Do not open it from the .dmg or from Downloads.
  3. macOS tells you that Primer comes from the internet. Click Open.
  4. In the setup guide, click Open Accessibility Settings.
  5. Turn on Primer in the list.
  6. Click Restart Primer. The step gets a green check.

Primer bounces in the Dock but does not open? A macOS message is open behind another window. Find it and answer it.

Accessibility gives an app wide control of your Mac. Primer uses it only to operate Logic during an import.

macOS can ask for access to Apple Music and your media library. That is your Music folder, where Logic keeps projects and templates. Primer only reads them. Click Allow.

Test

  1. Drop your files in Primer.
  2. Press Import to Logic. Logic closed or no project open? Select your template or an empty project.
  3. Do not touch the mouse or keyboard until Primer is done. To stop, press the red Stop button.
  4. Save the project in Logic.
  5. Check the project: auxes, names, colours and stripped silence.
  6. Send a report, also when everything worked. On the result screen, click Tell Us It Worked… or Bug Report. Then click Send Report.

On our test audio, 100 tracks took about 4.5 minutes.

A report contains your Primer, Mac, macOS and Logic versions, your Primer settings and the last steps in Logic. It contains no audio. You can also use the bug report page.

Updates

  1. Choose Primer > Check for Updates…
  2. Click Install Update. Primer restarts.

The setup guide asks for Accessibility again? Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility. Turn Primer off, then on.

Your files

Primer runs on your Mac. It only goes online when you click Check for Updates or Send Report. Primer works on copies, so your original files do not change. This is a test version: keep copies of your projects. More in the privacy notice.
