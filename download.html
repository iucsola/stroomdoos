Install

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Primer to Applications.
  2. Open Primer. If macOS asks you to confirm, click Open.
  3. In the setup guide, click Open Accessibility Settings and turn on Primer. Primer needs this to click through Logic for you.
  4. If macOS asks about Apple Music and your media library, click Allow. Logic keeps your templates in your Music folder, so Primer looks there to show them. It only reads them.

During an import, keep your hands off the mouse and keyboard. On our test audio, 100 tracks took about 4.5 minutes.

After your first import

Open Bug Report in Primer and click Send Report, also if everything worked. The report has no audio in it. What to test and how updates work: the beta guide.

Your files

Primer works on copies, so your original files stay as they are. It only goes online when you click Check for Updates or Send Report. This is a test version, so keep copies of your projects. More in the privacy notice.
