Test version

Primer is a free test version, so you may run into bugs. Primer leaves your original files alone, but save your Logic project before you import, just in case.

If something does go wrong, we can't take responsibility for lost work or data, as far as the law allows. Let us know what happened and we'll try to fix it.

Accessibility permission

Primer controls Logic Pro through macOS Accessibility, which gives the app broad control over your Mac while it runs. Only grant it if you're comfortable with that, and keep your hands off the mouse and keyboard during an import.
