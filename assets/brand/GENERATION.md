## S-woordmerk

Nieuwe generatie, transparante achtergrond:

> Design a single original, professional brand logo for a Dutch creative brand called "stroomdoos". Exact wordmark text: stroomdoos, all lowercase, spelled s t r o o m d o o s. Create a distinctive compact abstract S monogram suggesting a continuous electric current bending through a small box, paired on its right with a beautifully crafted bold lowercase geometric grotesk wordmark. Art direction: confident Dutch graphic design, practical industrial typography with a subtle playful personality, precisely balanced optical spacing, simple silhouette that works very small. Flat ink colors only: near-black #17191C for the wordmark and vivid vermilion orange #F04A2D for the icon. The icon must feel custom and identifiable, not a stock lightning bolt, not a plug illustration, not a generic app icon. One horizontal lockup, centered, generous clear space, crisp flat vector-like edges. Actual transparent background. No extra captions, no slogan, no mockups, no paper, no shadows, no gradients, no 3D, no presentation sheet, no multiple variations. This is the logo itself, high-resolution and clean.

A friendly confident professional Dutch graphic logo with an exceptionally simple silhouette. Solid vermilion #F04A2D, near-black #17191C and warm off-white #F1F0EA only. The cable has no connector tip; just one short round-ended bend. Socket pinholes are warm off-white. Precise clean edges and strong legibility at small sizes.
