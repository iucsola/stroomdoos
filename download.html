Install

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Primer to Applications.
  2. In Primer, the setup guide shows Open Accessibility Settings. Click it and turn Primer on.

A full import runs by itself and needs your hands off the mouse and keyboard. On our test audio, 100 tracks took about 4.5 minutes and 150 tracks about 7.

What you need, what to test and how updates work: the beta page.

Your files

Primer runs on your Mac and only goes online when you choose Check for Updates or press Send Report in a bug report. It works on copies and never changes your original files. This is a test version: keep copies of your projects. More in the privacy notice.
