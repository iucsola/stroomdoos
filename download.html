Install

  1. Open the .dmg and drag MixPrep to Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS says it can't check the app. That's expected: there's no Apple developer account behind it yet. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password.
  4. In MixPrep, follow the setup guide to turn on Accessibility.

What you need, what to test and how updates work: the beta page.

Your files

MixPrep runs on your Mac and only goes online when you choose Check for Updates. It works on copies and never changes your original files. This is a test version: keep copies of your projects. More in the privacy notice.
