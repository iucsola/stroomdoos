The Primer beta.

For beta testers. Install, test, send a report.

Not a tester? Join the mailing list and we'll tell you when Primer is out.

What you need

A Mac with macOS 14 or newer and Logic Pro 11+, set to English.

Install

  1. Download Primer, open the download and drag Primer to Applications.
  2. Open Primer. macOS asks if you're sure. Click Open.
  3. Turn on Primer in System Settings. Primer shows you where. If macOS asks about your media library, click Allow.

Test

  1. Drop your audio in Primer and press Import to Logic.
  2. Hands off the mouse and keyboard until Primer is done.
  3. Check the tracks in Logic.
  4. Click Tell Us It Worked… or Bug Report, then Send Report. No audio goes with it.

Updates

Choose Primer > Check for Updates…

Something wrong?

  • Primer bounces in the Dock but does not open: a macOS message is waiting behind another window. Answer it.
  • macOS says Primer is not from the App Store: in System Settings > Privacy & Security, set Allow applications from to App Store & Known Developers.
  • Primer asks for access again after an update: turn Primer off and on in System Settings.

Your files

Primer works on copies, so your originals do not change. It only goes online for updates and reports. This is a test version: keep copies of your projects. Privacy
