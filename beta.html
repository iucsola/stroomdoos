The Primer beta.

What you need

A Mac with macOS 14 or newer and Logic Pro 11+, set to English.

Install

  1. Download Primer, open the download and follow the installer.
  2. Open Primer. macOS asks if you're sure. Click Open.
  3. Turn on Primer in System Settings. Primer shows you where. If macOS asks about your media library, click Allow.

Something wrong?

  • macOS says Primer is not from the App Store: in System Settings > Privacy & Security, set Allow applications from to App Store & Known Developers.
  • Primer asks for access again after an update: turn Primer off and on in System Settings.
