Privacy

Short version: this site does not track you, and MixPrep keeps your audio on your Mac.

Who we are

Stroomdoos is Luc Siegers, the Netherlands. Contact: info@stroomdoos.com.

This website

No analytics, no tracking and no cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses to run the service. The typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Signing up as a tester

If you sign up as a tester on this site, your email address is stored in a mailing list at Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which processes it on our behalf. The sign-up request passes through Cloudflare, Inc. (United States), which forwards it to Brevo; Cloudflare only counts how many sign-ups succeeded or failed and does not store your address. If you email us instead, it stays in our mailbox. Either way, we use your email address only to share the test version, to send test updates and to answer you. We do not sell or share it. You can unsubscribe through the link in any email, or ask us any time to remove it. We keep your email address until you unsubscribe, and no longer than 12 months after the beta ends.

Bug reports

A bug report is only sent if you press Send Report in the app, or send the form on our bug report page. It contains the MixPrep version, your macOS version, your Mac's chip, the Logic Pro version, the steps MixPrep ran and the error lines from that run. If you add your email address, we keep it with the report so we can reply.

Reports are stored in a database run by Cloudflare, Inc. (United States) on our behalf. Only Luc reads them directly. We keep reports for up to 12 months after the end of the beta. You can ask us at any time to delete your report by emailing info@stroomdoos.com with the time you sent it.

The MixPrep app

MixPrep runs on your Mac. Your audio files, names and settings stay on your computer. MixPrep only goes online when you choose Check for Updates: it then reads the list of versions on this site and, if you install one, downloads it from GitHub. Like any download, GitHub sees your IP address and which MixPrep version asks. Nothing about you or your files is sent. A bug report is only sent if you press Send Report (see Bug reports below).

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Email info@stroomdoos.com.
