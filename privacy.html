Privacy

Short version: this site does not track you, and MixPrep keeps your audio on your Mac.

Who we are

Stroomdoos is Luc Siegers, the Netherlands. Contact: info@stroomdoos.com.

This website

No analytics, no tracking and no cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses to run the service. The typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Signing up as a tester

If you sign up as a tester on this site, your email address is stored in a mailing list at Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which processes it on our behalf. If you email us instead, it stays in our mailbox. Either way, we use your email address only to share the test version, to send test updates and to answer you. We do not sell or share it. You can unsubscribe through the link in any email, or ask us any time to remove it.

The MixPrep app

MixPrep runs on your Mac. Your audio files, names and settings stay on your computer. MixPrep only goes online when you choose Check for Updates: it then reads the list of versions on this site and, if you install one, downloads it from GitHub. Like any download, GitHub sees your IP address and which MixPrep version asks. The download button on our download page also fetches the file from GitHub. Nothing about you or your files is sent. A Bug Report is only sent if you choose to, through a Google Form, and it leaves out your file names.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Email info@stroomdoos.com.
