Install

  1. Open the .dmg. Drag Primer to Applications.
  2. Open Primer. In the setup guide, click Open Accessibility Settings. Turn on Primer.

During an import, do not touch the mouse or keyboard. On our test audio, 100 tracks took about 4.5 minutes.

All steps, what to test and how updates work: the beta guide.

Your files

Primer runs on your Mac. It only goes online when you click Check for Updates or Send Report. Primer works on copies, so your original files do not change. This is a test version: keep copies of your projects. More in the privacy notice.
