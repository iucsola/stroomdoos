Primer is a free test version, provided as is. It can contain bugs, so work on copies of your files and save your Logic projects before you import.

Accessibility permission

Primer controls Logic Pro through macOS Accessibility, which gives the app broad control over your Mac while it runs. Only grant it if you're comfortable with that, and keep your hands off the mouse and keyboard during an import.
