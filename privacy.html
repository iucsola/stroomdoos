Privacy

This site doesn't track you, and Primer keeps your audio on your Mac.

Who we are

Stroomdoos is Luc Siegers, in the Netherlands. Contact: info@stroomdoos.com.

This website

No analytics, no tracking, no cookies. The site runs on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as your IP address. The typeface comes from Google Fonts, which also sees your IP address when the page loads.

Signing up as a tester

When you sign up, your email address goes to our mailing list at Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France). The request passes through Cloudflare, Inc. (United States), which only counts how many sign-ups worked and doesn't store your address. If you email us instead, your address stays in our mailbox. We use it to send you the test version and updates, and to answer you. We don't sell or share it. You can unsubscribe through any email, or ask us to remove it. We keep your address until you unsubscribe, and at most 12 months after the beta ends.

Bug reports

A bug report is only sent when you press Send Report in the app or submit the form on our bug report page. It holds technical details about your Mac, macOS, Logic Pro and Primer, how Primer sorted your files, the log of the last run, and anything you type yourself. It contains no audio. If you add your email address, we keep it with the report so we can reply.

Reports are stored in a Cloudflare, Inc. database (United States). For that transfer, Cloudflare relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses as a fallback. Luc reads them directly. We keep reports for up to 12 months after the beta ends. To have yours deleted, email info@stroomdoos.com with the time you sent it.

The Primer app

Primer runs on your Mac, and your audio files stay there. The app only goes online when you choose Check for Updates or send a bug report. An update check reads the list of versions on this site and, if you install one, downloads it from GitHub. As with any download, GitHub sees your IP address and which version asks. The download button on our download page works the same way. Nothing about you or your files is sent, apart from what a bug report contains.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Email info@stroomdoos.com.
