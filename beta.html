The MixPrep beta.

MixPrep puts your multitrack WAVs into Logic Pro in one go: each file on its own track, named, coloured and routed to an aux per group. It's in beta, and every Mac and Logic version helps.

Signed up? The download link comes by email. Not yet? Sign up here.

What you need

  • A Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel) with macOS 14 or newer.
  • Logic Pro set to English. Tested on 12.4; other versions are very welcome.
  • Copies of a real session's WAVs, not your only version.

Install

  1. Open the .dmg and drag MixPrep to Applications.
  2. In MixPrep, the setup guide shows Open Accessibility Settings. Click it, turn MixPrep on in the list and go back: the step gets a green check. That's how MixPrep operates Logic for you.

MixPrep icon bouncing in the Dock and nothing opens? A macOS message is waiting, probably behind another window.

Accessibility gives an app broad control over your Mac. MixPrep only uses it to operate Logic during an import.

Test

  1. Open a new project from your template in Logic (File > New from Template) and save it.
  2. Drop your files in MixPrep and press Import to Logic.
  3. Hands off the mouse and keyboard until it's done. The red Stop button stops it.
  4. Look in Logic: right auxes? Names? Colours? Silence stripped?
  5. Click Bug Report in MixPrep, then Send Report, also when everything worked. The report has your MixPrep, Mac, macOS and Logic version and the last steps in Logic. It contains no audio. You can also send it from the bug report page.

Updates

Choose MixPrep > Check for Updates… and click Install Update: MixPrep restarts by itself. Or by hand: quit MixPrep, drag the new version from the .dmg to Applications and choose Replace. The first time macOS may ask again (see Install). If MixPrep asks for Accessibility again, switch it off and on in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.

Your files

MixPrep runs on your Mac and only goes online when you choose Check for Updates or press Send Report in a bug report. It works on copies and never changes your original files. This is a test version: keep copies of your projects. More in the privacy notice.
