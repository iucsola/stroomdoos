The Primer beta.

Primer puts your multitrack WAVs into Logic Pro in one go: each file on its own track, named, coloured and routed to an aux per group.

Signed up? The download link comes by email. Not yet? Sign up here.

What you need

  • A Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel) with macOS 14 or newer.
  • Logic Pro set to English. Tested on 12 and 13; other versions are very welcome.
  • A few audio files or a massive multitrack, up to you!

Install

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Primer to Applications. Open it from there, not from the .dmg or Downloads.
  2. The first time, macOS says Primer was downloaded from the internet and checked by Apple. Click Open.
  3. In Primer, the setup guide shows Open Accessibility Settings. Click it and turn Primer on in the list. Primer asks to restart: click Restart Primer. Back in Primer, the step gets a green check. That's how Primer operates Logic for you.

Primer icon bouncing in the Dock and nothing opens? A macOS message is waiting, probably behind another window.

Accessibility gives an app broad control over your Mac. Primer only uses it to operate Logic during an import.

macOS may also ask if Primer can access Apple Music and your media library. That's how macOS names your Music folder, where Logic keeps its projects and templates. Primer only reads them. Click Allow.

Test

  1. Drop your files in Primer and press Import to Logic. Logic closed or no project open? Primer asks where the tracks go: your template or an empty project.
  2. Hands off the mouse and keyboard until it's done. The red Stop button stops it. On our test audio, 100 tracks took about 4.5 minutes and 150 tracks about 7.
  3. Save the project in Logic.
  4. Look in Logic: right auxes? Names? Colours? Silence stripped?
  5. Send us a report, also when everything worked: click Tell Us It Worked… on the result screen, or Bug Report when something went wrong, then Send Report. The report has your Primer, Mac, macOS and Logic version, your Primer settings and the last steps in Logic. It contains no audio. You can also use the bug report page.

Updates

Choose Primer > Check for Updates… and click Install Update: Primer restarts by itself. Or by hand: quit Primer, drag the new version from the .dmg to Applications and choose Replace. The first time macOS may ask again (see Install). If the setup guide asks for Accessibility again, switch Primer off and on in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.

Your files

Primer runs on your Mac and only goes online when you choose Check for Updates or press Send Report in a bug report. It works on copies and never changes your original files. This is a test version: keep copies of your projects. More in the privacy notice.
